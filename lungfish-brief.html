Mary River Lungfish Brief

Neoceratodus forsteri — living fossil, endemic, protected.

Why it matters

The Mary River lungfish is an ancient lineage found only in a handful of south-east Queensland rivers. Yabba Creek and the upper Mary are core habitat.

Borumba threat

  • Reservoir and dam works on Yabba Creek risk direct habitat loss and fragmentation
  • Sediment, temperature, and flow changes threaten breeding and survival
  • Downstream effects on the Mary River system and Great Sandy Strait
  • Cumulative pressure alongside existing dams and extraction

Position

Any project that further stresses the lungfish on Kabi Country fails the test of protecting Country and its living kin. Mine-site and closed-loop alternatives avoid this destruction.

Heal the scars. Do not drown Yabba. Protect the lungfish.
